How to File an Accident Insurance Claim with American Fidelity

After an accidental injury, get medical treatment first. Then gather your medical records and injury documentation, sign in to the portal, select all applicable accident benefits (fracture, ER visit, surgery, etc.), and submit with documentation. Remember — benefits stack, so one accident can trigger multiple payments.

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Get Medical Treatment

Your health and safety come first. Visit the emergency room, urgent care, or your doctor. Keep all medical documentation — you will need it for your claim. Note the date and time of the accident and how it happened.

Step 2: Gather Documentation

Collect:

  • Medical records — ER records, doctor's notes, discharge summaries
  • Diagnostic reports — X-rays, CT scans, MRI results (especially for fractures)
  • Treatment records — surgical reports, physical therapy records
  • Accident details — date, time, location, and circumstances
  • Follow-up visit records — each follow-up may be a separate benefit

Step 3: Sign In and Select Accident Claim

Sign in at secured-americanfidelity.com, go to Claims, and select Accident Insurance.

Step 4: Describe the Accident and Select Benefits

Describe when, where, and how the accident happened. Then select all applicable benefits from your plan's schedule — fracture, dislocation, ER visit, hospital admission, ambulance, stitches, surgery, and follow-up visits. Each covered event is a separate benefit.

Step 5: Upload and Submit

Attach all medical documentation, review for accuracy, and submit. Track your claim through the Claims section of the portal.

Maximizing Your Claim

Accident insurance pays per covered event, and benefits stack. A single accident might trigger ER visit + X-ray + fracture + ambulance + follow-up visit benefits. Review your plan's full benefit schedule and claim every applicable event.
  • Claim every covered event — don't overlook follow-up visits and physical therapy
  • File follow-up claims as they occur — each PT session or follow-up visit can be claimed
  • Check if family members are covered — your plan may cover spouse and children's accidents too
  • File within your plan's deadline — typically 90 days from the date of accident
