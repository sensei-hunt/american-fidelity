American Fidelity Accident Insurance: Coverage for Unexpected Injuries

American Fidelity accident insurance pays cash benefits when you or a covered family member suffers an accidental injury. Benefits cover specific events like fractures, dislocations, emergency room visits, surgery, and follow-up care — paid directly to you regardless of other insurance.

Who Benefits Most from Accident Insurance?

Accident insurance is especially valuable for active individuals, families with children in sports, employees with high-deductible health plans, and anyone who wants financial protection against the unexpected costs of accidental injuries.
  • Active adults — runners, cyclists, gym-goers, weekend athletes
  • Parents of children in sports — football, soccer, gymnastics, cheerleading
  • HDHP enrollees — bridges the gap before you meet your deductible
  • Outdoor workers — construction, landscaping, agriculture
  • Anyone without an emergency fund — immediate cash for unexpected injuries

How Accident Insurance Pays

Accident insurance uses a schedule of benefits — a list of covered events with fixed dollar amounts. When you have a covered accident, you file a claim and receive the scheduled benefit. Benefits stack: a single accident that involves an ER visit, X-ray, fracture, and follow-up visits triggers multiple benefit payments.

Example Scenario

You break your arm playing basketball and go to the ER:

  • Emergency room visit: $200
  • X-ray/diagnostic: $50
  • Fracture (arm): $1,200
  • Follow-up visits (3): $225
  • Physical therapy (6 sessions): $300
  • Total payout: $1,975
