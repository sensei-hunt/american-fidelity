How to File a Cancer Insurance Claim with American Fidelity

To file a cancer insurance claim, gather your pathology report and treatment records, sign in to the portal, select the cancer claim type (diagnosis, treatment, or screening), complete the form, and upload documentation. Most claims are processed within 5–10 business days.

Types of Cancer Claims

You can file separate claims for each covered event:

  • Initial Diagnosis Claim — lump-sum benefit upon first cancer diagnosis (requires pathology report)
  • Treatment Claims — per-treatment benefits for chemotherapy, radiation, surgery (requires treatment records)
  • Hospital Confinement Claim — daily benefit for inpatient stays during cancer treatment
  • Wellness Screening Claim — annual benefit for cancer screenings (mammogram, colonoscopy, PSA, etc.)

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Confirm Your Coverage

Sign in and check that your cancer insurance policy is active. Review your benefit schedule to understand what's covered and the benefit amounts for each event type.

Step 2: Obtain Medical Documentation

For a diagnosis claim: pathology report confirming the cancer diagnosis, date of diagnosis, and type/stage of cancer. For treatment claims: treatment records showing dates, type (chemo, radiation, surgery), and provider information. For wellness screening: receipt or explanation of benefits showing the screening was performed.

Step 3: Start Your Claim Online

Sign in to secured-americanfidelity.com, go to Claims, and select Cancer Insurance. Choose the specific benefit type you are claiming.

Step 4: Complete the Form

Fill out the claim form with your diagnosis details, treating oncologist information, treatment dates, and hospital information (if applicable). Be as specific as possible about dates and providers.

Step 5: Upload Documentation

Attach all supporting documents. For diagnosis claims, the pathology report is required. For treatment claims, attach treatment summaries or records from your oncology provider.

Step 6: Submit and Track

Submit your claim, note the confirmation number, and monitor status in the Claims section. You can file treatment claims as each session occurs — you don't need to wait until all treatment is complete.

Filing Tips

  • File the diagnosis claim first — this is typically the largest benefit amount
  • File treatment claims as you go — submit after each chemo/radiation session or surgery
  • Don't forget wellness screening claims — many policyholders miss this annual benefit
  • Keep a claims log — track which treatments you've claimed to avoid missing any
