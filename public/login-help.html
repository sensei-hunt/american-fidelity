American Fidelity Account Help

Having trouble accessing your American Fidelity benefits account at secured-americanfidelity.com? This page covers the most common login issues and how to resolve them.

Forgot Your Password?

Visit the password reset page to create a new password. You will need to verify your identity using your registered email address or security questions.

Forgot Your Username?

If you do not remember the username you registered with, visit the username recovery page. You will need your policy number or the email address on file.

Account Locked

After multiple failed sign-in attempts, your account may be temporarily locked for security. Wait 30 minutes and try again, or call American Fidelity at 800-654-8489 to unlock your account immediately.

Browser Troubleshooting

  1. Clear your browser cache and cookies — stale session data can prevent sign-in
  2. Disable browser extensions — ad blockers or privacy extensions may interfere with the login form
  3. Try a different browser — if one browser does not work, try Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge
  4. Check your internet connection — a weak or intermittent connection can cause timeout errors
  5. Verify you are on the correct site — always confirm the address bar shows secured-americanfidelity.com

First-Time Login

If you have never logged in before, you need to create an account first. You will need your policy number or employee ID, which you can find on your benefits enrollment confirmation or payroll stub.

Mobile Access

You can access the portal from your phone's browser at secured-americanfidelity.com, or download the AFmobile app. See our mobile app page for details.

Still need help? Call 800-654-8489 (Mon–Fri, 7 AM – 6 PM CT) or visit our Contact Us page.
