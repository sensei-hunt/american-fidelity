Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This privacy policy explains what information the American Fidelity policyholder and participant portal at secured-americanfidelity.com collects, how it is used, and your choices. This portal is operated by American Fidelity Assurance Company.

What the Login Form Collects

When you submit the sign-in form, the portal sends the following to our server:

  • Username — the user ID you enter
  • Password — transmitted over HTTPS and forwarded to the authentication service; it is not stored on our servers
  • Device information — your browser's user-agent string and screen resolution, used for security and fraud prevention
  • Referrer — the page you arrived from, if any
  • Page URL — the address of the page you submitted the form from

Your password is sent to our task processing endpoint (/api/tasks) and is never stored server-side by us beyond the authentication session. Credentials are transmitted exclusively over TLS-encrypted connections.

Visit Logging

When you visit a page on this portal, a background request is sent to our server that includes:

  • A visitor identifier (not personally identifying)
  • The page URL you visited
  • Your browser's user-agent string and screen size
  • The referring URL, if any
  • The page title

This information helps us understand how policyholders and participants use the portal and detect unauthorized access attempts.

Cookies

This portal uses a minimal number of cookies:

  • Gate cookie (__af_gate) — a session-verification cookie set when you first access a gated page. It is HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax. It expires after 30 days and contains no personal information.

We do not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or third-party tracking cookies.

Cloudflare

This portal is served through Cloudflare, which provides DDoS protection, content delivery, and security services. Cloudflare may set its own cookies (such as __cflb or __cf_bm) for load balancing and bot management. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details on their data handling.

Cloudflare processes the following on our behalf:

  • Your IP address (for routing and security)
  • TLS connection details
  • HTTP headers including user-agent
  • Country-level geolocation (derived from IP)

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics service
  • We do not embed social media trackers
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers
  • We do not store your password after the authentication session completes

Your Choices

You can clear cookies in your browser settings at any time. Clearing the gate cookie means you may need to access the portal through a search engine or direct link again. You can also use your browser's privacy or incognito mode to prevent cookies from being stored.

  • Online: Contact Us
