Upload Claim Documents to American Fidelity

After filing a claim, you may need to submit supporting documents such as medical records, receipts, employer statements, or completed forms. You can upload these through your online account.

How to Upload Documents Online

  1. Sign in to your American Fidelity benefits account
  2. Go to the Claims section and select your open claim
  3. Click Upload Documents
  4. Select the file(s) from your device — accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG
  5. Add a brief description of what the document is
  6. Submit

What Documents Are Typically Needed?

  • Disability claims: attending physician statement, employer statement of earnings, medical records
  • Cancer claims: pathology report, treatment records, diagnosis confirmation from your oncologist
  • Accident claims: emergency room or urgent care records, X-ray reports, receipts
  • Hospital indemnity: hospital admission and discharge records
  • Life insurance: certified death certificate, claimant statement, beneficiary identification

Tips for Uploading

  • Make sure documents are legible — avoid blurry photos
  • Each file should be under 10 MB
  • If you have multiple pages, combine them into one PDF when possible
  • Upload all documents at once to avoid processing delays

Other Ways to Submit Documents

If you cannot upload online:

  • Fax: contact American Fidelity for the claims fax number
  • Mail: American Fidelity Assurance Company, P.O. Box 25523, Oklahoma City, OK 73125

Need help? Call 800-654-8489 (Mon–Fri, 7 AM – 6 PM CT).
