American Fidelity Employer Login

The employer portal is for HR administrators, benefits coordinators, and authorized employer representatives who manage American Fidelity benefit programs for their organization.

What Employers Can Do

  • Manage enrollment — add, remove, or update employee benefit elections
  • View billing — access premium statements and payment history
  • Download reports — pull enrollment, claims, and utilization reports
  • Update employee information — process life events, terminations, and demographic changes
  • Access plan documents — download certificates, SPDs, and enrollment materials

How to Get Employer Access

Employer portal access is set up through your American Fidelity account manager. If you do not have an account manager assigned, or you need to request access for a new administrator:

  1. Call 800-654-8489 and ask for Employer Services
  2. Provide your organization name and group policy number
  3. The account manager will set up your credentials and walk you through the portal

Are You a Policyholder or Participant?

If you are an employee looking to access your own benefits account, use the policyholder and participant sign-in instead. The employer portal is only for authorized administrators.

Questions about employer administration? Call 800-654-8489 (Mon–Fri, 7 AM – 6 PM CT) or contact your American Fidelity account manager directly.
