Online Security – Protecting Your Account

American Fidelity is committed to protecting your personal and financial information. We use industry-leading security measures and encourage you to follow best practices to keep your account safe.

How We Protect You

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is fully encrypted
  • Multi-Factor Authentication: Additional verification steps to confirm your identity
  • Automatic Session Timeout: Sessions end after inactivity to prevent unauthorized access
  • Encrypted Data Storage: Personal and policy data is stored using enterprise-grade encryption
  • 24/7 Monitoring: Continuous monitoring for suspicious activity and unauthorized access attempts
  • Regular Security Audits: Third-party security assessments to identify and address vulnerabilities

Protecting Your Account

Use a strong, unique password. At least 12 characters with a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse passwords from other sites.
Enable multi-factor authentication. Add a second verification step to make unauthorized access significantly harder.
Never share your login credentials. American Fidelity staff will never ask for your password by phone, email, or text.
Use trusted devices and networks. Avoid public Wi-Fi when accessing your account. Use a VPN if you must connect from a public network.
Keep your software updated. Use the latest version of your browser and operating system to stay protected against known vulnerabilities.

Recognizing Phishing & Scams

Be cautious of:

  • Emails creating urgency ("Your account will be suspended!")
  • Links asking you to verify your account or update personal information
  • Messages from unfamiliar email addresses claiming to be American Fidelity
  • Requests for your password, Social Security number, or policy details via email or text
Important: American Fidelity will never send unsolicited emails asking for your password or Social Security number. If you receive a suspicious message, do not click any links. Forward it to support@secured-americanfidelity.com and delete it.

Reporting Suspicious Activity

If you notice unauthorized transactions, unfamiliar login attempts, or account changes you didn't make:

  • Change your password immediately
  • Call 800-654-8489 to report the activity
  • Review your recent account activity for unauthorized changes
  • Consider placing a fraud alert with the major credit bureaus if identity theft is suspected

Want to update your security settings? Sign in to your account to enable multi-factor authentication and manage your preferences.
