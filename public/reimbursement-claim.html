File a Reimbursement Claim with American Fidelity

If your American Fidelity supplemental insurance plan includes reimbursement benefits, you can file a claim to recover eligible out-of-pocket expenses. This applies to certain cancer, accident, hospital indemnity, and critical illness plans that reimburse policyholders for covered costs.

How to File a Reimbursement Claim

  1. Sign in to your American Fidelity benefits account
  2. Go to the Claims section and select File a New Claim
  3. Choose your policy and select Reimbursement as the claim type
  4. Enter the date of service, provider name, and amount
  5. Upload itemized receipts or an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) from your primary insurance
  6. Submit the claim

What Qualifies for Reimbursement?

Eligible expenses depend on your specific plan. Common reimbursable items include:

  • Cancer insurance: treatment copays, transportation to treatment, lodging, prescription drugs
  • Accident insurance: emergency room copays, follow-up visits, physical therapy
  • Hospital indemnity: hospital admission fees, outpatient surgery costs

Check your policy certificate or summary plan description for the full list of covered expenses and limits.

Required Documentation

  • Itemized receipt showing the date, provider, service, and amount paid
  • Explanation of Benefits (EOB) from your primary health insurer, if applicable
  • Proof of payment (credit card statement or canceled check) if no receipt is available

How Long Does Reimbursement Take?

Reimbursement claims are typically processed within 5–15 business days after all documentation is received. Set up direct deposit to receive payment faster.

Questions about what is covered? Call 800-654-8489 (Mon–Fri, 7 AM – 6 PM CT) or review your policy documents in your online account.
