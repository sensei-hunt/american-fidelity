AFmobile — American Fidelity Mobile App

Access your American Fidelity benefits from your phone with the AFmobile app. View policy details, file claims, check claim status, and find your digital ID card — all from your mobile device.

What You Can Do with AFmobile

  • View your policies — see coverage details, benefit amounts, and effective dates
  • File a claim — submit claims directly from your phone with photo attachments
  • Check claim status — track where your claim is in the review process
  • Access your ID card — show your digital insurance card at the doctor's office
  • Update your information — change your address, beneficiary, or contact details
  • Contact support — call or email American Fidelity directly from the app

Download AFmobile

The AFmobile app is available for both iPhone and Android:

  • iPhone/iPad: Search "AFmobile" in the App Store, or look for "American Fidelity" in the Health & Fitness category
  • Android: Search "AFmobile" in the Google Play Store

Sign In to AFmobile

Use the same username and password you use to sign in at secured-americanfidelity.com. If you do not have an account yet, register online first, then sign in to the app with those credentials.

Troubleshooting

  • Cannot sign in? Make sure you can sign in on the website first. If not, visit Account Help
  • App not loading? Check for app updates in your device's app store
  • Forgot password? Reset it here, then sign in to the app

Prefer the web? You can always access your account at secured-americanfidelity.com from any mobile browser.
