Is American Fidelity the Same as Fidelity?

No. American Fidelity Assurance Company and Fidelity Investments are two entirely separate, unrelated companies. American Fidelity is an Oklahoma City-based insurance company that provides supplemental employee benefits. Fidelity Investments is a Boston-based financial services firm that manages investment accounts, retirement plans, and brokerage services.

What American Fidelity Does

American Fidelity Assurance Company specializes in supplemental insurance products offered through employer benefit programs. Their core products include:

  • Disability insurance (short-term and long-term)
  • Cancer insurance
  • Accident insurance
  • Critical illness insurance
  • Hospital indemnity insurance
  • Life insurance and AD&D
  • Section 125 cafeteria plan administration

Why the Confusion?

The similar name leads to frequent confusion, but the two companies have no corporate relationship, no shared ownership, and no business partnership. If your employer offers supplemental insurance through "American Fidelity," you're dealing with American Fidelity Assurance Company — not the investment firm.

How to Tell Which One You Need

  • If you need to manage supplemental insurance, file a claim, or enroll in employee benefits → You need American Fidelity at secured-americanfidelity.com
  • If you need to manage investments, retirement accounts, or brokerage accounts → You need Fidelity Investments at fidelity.com
