American Fidelity Cancer Insurance: Diagnosis & Treatment Benefits

American Fidelity cancer insurance pays cash benefits directly to you — not your doctor or hospital — when you are diagnosed with cancer or receive cancer treatment. Benefits are paid regardless of what your major medical plan covers, giving you money to use however you need.

What Does Cancer Insurance Cover?

Cancer insurance is a supplemental policy that provides financial support at every stage, from initial screening through treatment and recovery. Benefits are structured as fixed-dollar amounts for specific events:

Why Do You Need Cancer Insurance?

Even with good health insurance, a cancer diagnosis creates significant out-of-pocket costs. Cancer insurance helps cover:

  • Medical expenses — deductibles, copays, and coinsurance your health plan doesn't cover
  • Travel costs — transportation to treatment centers, lodging during treatment
  • Lost income — reduced hours or unpaid leave during treatment
  • Household expenses — mortgage, utilities, and everyday bills that continue during treatment
  • Childcare — additional care needs while a parent is in treatment

Types of Cancer Covered

American Fidelity cancer insurance covers all types of internal cancer and invasive melanoma. Coverage details for skin cancer and carcinoma in situ (early-stage cancer) vary by plan. Common covered diagnoses include:

  • Breast cancer, lung cancer, colon cancer, prostate cancer
  • Leukemia, lymphoma, and blood cancers
  • Ovarian, uterine, and cervical cancers
  • Bladder, kidney, liver, and pancreatic cancers
  • Brain and central nervous system tumors
  • Invasive melanoma (skin cancer terms vary by plan)

How Cancer Insurance Works with Health Insurance

Cancer insurance pays cash benefits directly to you, separate from your health insurance. Your health plan covers medical bills; cancer insurance gives you cash to cover everything else — deductibles, lost income, travel, childcare, and daily expenses.

There is no coordination of benefits. You receive the full cancer insurance benefit amount regardless of what your medical plan pays. Benefits are paid upon diagnosis or treatment, not based on actual medical bills.

Enrollment & Eligibility

Cancer insurance is available during your employer's open enrollment period. Coverage is typically offered for employees, spouses, and dependent children. Pre-existing condition clauses may apply — review your plan documents for specific terms.
