Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to my American Fidelity benefits account?

Go to secured-americanfidelity.com and enter your username and password in the Policyholders & Participants section. After signing in, you can view your policies, file claims, and manage your benefits.

What types of insurance does American Fidelity offer?

American Fidelity offers supplemental insurance including disability insurance (short-term and long-term), life insurance, cancer insurance, accident insurance, critical illness insurance, and hospital indemnity plans — all offered through employer-sponsored benefit programs.

How do I file a claim with American Fidelity?

Sign in to your account at secured-americanfidelity.com, navigate to the claims section, and follow the guided process to submit your claim. You will need documentation such as medical records or receipts depending on the type of claim. See our Claims Help page for detailed instructions.

When is open enrollment for American Fidelity benefits?

Open enrollment periods vary by employer. Check with your HR department or sign in to your portal for specific enrollment dates and available benefit options. Learn more on our Open Enrollment Guide.

I forgot my username or password. What do I do?

Use the password reset option on the sign-in page. You will need to verify your identity before you can reset your credentials and regain access to your account.

Is this the official American Fidelity website?

Yes — this is the official American Fidelity policyholder and participant portal. Always confirm you are on secured-americanfidelity.com before entering your credentials, and be wary of look-alike domains.
