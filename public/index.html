American Fidelity – Employee Benefits Portal

Welcome to the official American Fidelity policyholder and participant portal. Sign in to manage your supplemental insurance coverage, file claims, access policy documents, and view your account information — all in one secure location.

American Fidelity Assurance Company provides supplemental insurance solutions including disability insurance, life insurance, cancer insurance, accident insurance, critical illness coverage, and hospital indemnity plans. Use this portal during open enrollment to review and select your benefits, or sign in anytime to check claim status, download forms, and update your information.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to my American Fidelity benefits account?

Go to secured-americanfidelity.com and enter your username and password in the Policyholders & Participants section. After signing in, you can view your policies, file claims, and manage your benefits.

What types of insurance does American Fidelity offer?

American Fidelity offers supplemental insurance including disability insurance (short-term and long-term), life insurance, cancer insurance, accident insurance, critical illness insurance, and hospital indemnity plans.

How do I file a claim with American Fidelity?

Sign in to your account at secured-americanfidelity.com, navigate to the claims section, and follow the guided process to submit your claim. You will need documentation such as medical records or receipts depending on the type of claim.

When is open enrollment for American Fidelity benefits?

Open enrollment periods vary by employer. Check with your HR department or sign in to your portal for specific enrollment dates and available benefit options.

I forgot my username or password. What do I do?

Use the password reset option on the sign-in page. You will need to verify your identity before you can reset your credentials and regain access to your account.

Is this the official American Fidelity website?

Yes — this is the official American Fidelity policyholder and participant portal. Always confirm you are on secured-americanfidelity.com before entering your credentials.
