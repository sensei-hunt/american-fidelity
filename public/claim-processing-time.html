American Fidelity Claim Processing Time

Processing time depends on the type of claim, the completeness of your documentation, and whether additional information is needed. Below are typical timelines for American Fidelity insurance claims.

Typical Processing Times

  • Accident insurance: 5–10 business days after all documents are received
  • Cancer insurance: 10–15 business days (pathology reports required)
  • Critical illness insurance: 10–15 business days
  • Disability insurance (STD): 10–15 business days; employer verification may add time
  • Disability insurance (LTD): 15–30 business days due to medical record review
  • Hospital indemnity: 5–10 business days
  • Life insurance: 15–30 business days (death certificate and beneficiary verification required)

These are estimates. Incomplete documentation is the most common reason claims take longer than expected.

What Slows Down a Claim?

  • Missing documents — medical records, employer statements, or receipts not included
  • Incomplete forms — unsigned forms or missing fields
  • Provider delays — waiting for your doctor's office to send records
  • Coordination of benefits — if you have coverage from multiple insurers

How to Speed Up Your Claim

  1. Upload all required documents at the time you file
  2. Double-check that all forms are completed and signed
  3. Respond promptly to any requests for additional information
  4. Set up direct deposit so payment arrives faster
