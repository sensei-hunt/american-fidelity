About American Fidelity Assurance Company

American Fidelity Assurance Company is a privately held supplemental insurance provider headquartered in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. Founded in 1960, the company specializes in employer-sponsored voluntary benefits for education, public sector, and healthcare employees, including disability, cancer, accident, critical illness, hospital indemnity, and life insurance.

Company Overview

  • Founded: 1960
  • Headquarters: Oklahoma City, Oklahoma
  • Ownership: Privately held
  • Industry: Supplemental insurance / Employee benefits
  • Primary Markets: Education (K-12, higher ed), public sector, healthcare
  • Products: Disability, cancer, accident, critical illness, hospital indemnity, life insurance, Section 125 administration
  • Customer Service: 800-654-8489

What American Fidelity Does

American Fidelity partners with employers to offer supplemental insurance benefits that protect employees against income loss and unexpected medical expenses. The company provides:

  • Disability insurance — short-term and long-term income replacement
  • Cancer insurance — diagnosis and treatment cash benefits
  • Accident insurance — injury and treatment cash benefits
  • Critical illness insurance — lump-sum benefits for major conditions
  • Hospital indemnity insurance — daily cash for hospital stays
  • Life insurance & AD&D — death and dismemberment benefits
  • Section 125 plan administration — pre-tax benefits management

Not the Same as Fidelity Investments

American Fidelity Assurance Company is not affiliated with Fidelity Investments. They are separate, unrelated companies. American Fidelity is an insurance company; Fidelity Investments manages investment and retirement accounts. Read the full comparison →

Employer Partnership

American Fidelity works directly with employers to design and deliver voluntary benefit programs. This includes benefits education, enrollment support through dedicated benefits counselors, Section 125 plan setup and administration, and ongoing claims and customer service.
