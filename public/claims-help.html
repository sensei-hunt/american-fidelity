How to File a Claim with American Fidelity

Filing a claim with American Fidelity is straightforward. Whether you've experienced an accident, received a medical diagnosis, or need to file a disability claim, we're here to help you through the process. Most claims can be submitted online through your account portal for the fastest processing.

Before You File

Gather the following documents before starting your claim:

  • Your American Fidelity policy number (found on your benefits card or policy documents)
  • Date of the incident, diagnosis, or first day of disability
  • Attending physician's name and contact information
  • Medical records or documentation related to your claim
  • Itemized bills or Explanation of Benefits (EOB) from your primary insurance

Step-by-Step Claims Process

Step 1: Log In to Your Account

Sign in to your American Fidelity account and navigate to the Claims section. If you don't have an online account, you can register or call us to file by phone.

Step 2: Select Your Claim Type

Choose the type of claim you're filing: Disability, Cancer, Accident, Hospital Indemnity, or Life Insurance. Each claim type has specific documentation requirements.

Step 3: Complete the Claim Form

Fill out all required fields accurately. Provide details about the event, treatment received, and any healthcare providers involved. Incomplete forms may delay processing.

Step 4: Upload Supporting Documents

Attach copies of medical records, bills, and any other relevant documentation. Clear, legible copies ensure faster review. Accepted formats include PDF, JPG, and PNG.

Step 5: Submit and Track

Review your claim for accuracy, then submit. You'll receive a confirmation number and can track your claim status online at any time through your account dashboard.

Claims Processing Timeline

  • Accident claims: Typically processed within 5-10 business days
  • Disability claims: Initial review within 10-15 business days
  • Cancer claims: Typically processed within 10-15 business days
  • Life insurance claims: Typically processed within 15-30 business days

Processing times may vary based on the complexity of your claim and completeness of submitted documentation.

Claim Payment Options

Once your claim is approved, benefits can be paid by:

  • Direct deposit — fastest option, typically received within 2-3 business days after approval
  • Paper check — mailed to your address on file, typically arrives within 7-10 business days

If Your Claim Is Denied

If your claim is denied, you will receive a written explanation of the reason. You have the right to appeal the decision. To file an appeal:

  1. Review the denial letter carefully to understand the specific reason
  2. Gather any additional documentation that supports your claim
  3. Submit a written appeal within 60 days of the denial, including new supporting evidence
  4. Contact our claims department at 800-654-8489 if you need guidance

Ready to file? Sign in to your account to start your claim online, or call 800-654-8489 for assistance.
