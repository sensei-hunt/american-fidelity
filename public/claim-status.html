Check Your American Fidelity Claim Status

After filing a claim with American Fidelity, you can check its status online through your benefits account at secured-americanfidelity.com.

How to Check Claim Status Online

  1. Sign in to your American Fidelity benefits account
  2. Navigate to the Claims section
  3. Select the claim you want to check
  4. View the current status, any requested documents, and payment information

Claim Status Stages

  • Received — your claim has been received and assigned to a claims examiner
  • Under Review — the examiner is reviewing your claim and supporting documents
  • Additional Information Needed — you need to provide more documentation (check for details)
  • Approved — your claim has been approved and payment is being processed
  • Paid — payment has been sent via your chosen method (check or direct deposit)
  • Denied — your claim was not approved (you have the right to appeal)

How Long Does It Take?

Processing times vary by claim type. See our claim processing time page for detailed timelines. If your claim has been under review for longer than expected, call 800-654-8489.

Cannot find your claim? Make sure you are signed in with the account that filed the claim. Call 800-654-8489 if you need help locating it.
