American Fidelity Hospital Indemnity Insurance: Daily Cash Benefits

American Fidelity hospital indemnity insurance pays a fixed daily cash benefit for every day you are confined in a hospital, plus additional benefits for admission and ICU stays. Benefits are paid directly to you regardless of what your health insurance covers.

How Hospital Indemnity Insurance Works

Hospital indemnity is a supplemental policy that pays you cash when you are admitted to a hospital. Unlike major medical insurance that pays your healthcare provider, hospital indemnity pays you a set dollar amount per day of confinement. You decide how to spend it.

Example Payout

If you are hospitalized for 4 days with a plan that pays $1,000 admission + $300/day confinement:

  • Admission benefit: $1,000
  • Daily confinement: $300 × 4 days = $1,200
  • Total payout: $2,200 — paid directly to you

Why Hospital Indemnity Matters

The average hospital stay in the United States costs thousands of dollars in out-of-pocket expenses even with health insurance. Hospital indemnity helps bridge the gap between what your health plan pays and what you owe — plus everyday bills that pile up while you recover.
  • Covers high-deductible health plan (HDHP) gaps
  • Helps pay mortgage, rent, and utilities during recovery
  • Supplements lost income from missed work
  • No network restrictions — any licensed hospital qualifies
  • No coordination with other insurance

Hospital Indemnity vs. Other Products

vs. Critical Illness: Critical illness pays a one-time lump sum on diagnosis of specific conditions. Hospital indemnity pays for any hospital stay, regardless of the reason.

vs. Disability: Disability replaces income when you can't work. Hospital indemnity pays when you're hospitalized, whether or not you miss work after discharge.

vs. Accident: Accident insurance covers injuries from accidents only. Hospital indemnity covers hospitalization from any cause — illness or accident.

Enrollment

Enroll during open enrollment through your employer. Coverage is available for employees, spouses, and dependents. Premiums are based on age and coverage tier, with optional pre-tax payroll deductions under a Section 125 plan.
