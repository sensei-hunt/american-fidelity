American Fidelity Employee Benefits Portal – Insurance Login & Claims Management

Welcome to the official American Fidelity employee benefits portal at secured-americanfidelity.com. This is your secure online destination for managing your supplemental insurance benefits, filing claims, enrolling in coverage, accessing policy documents, and contacting support.

Quick Access: Sign in to your account to file a claim, check claim status, view your coverage, or manage your benefits. New employee? Register for an account.

What Is the American Fidelity Benefits Portal?

The American Fidelity benefits portal at secured-americanfidelity.com is a comprehensive online platform that gives employees full access to their supplemental insurance benefits. Through this portal, you can:

  • File Insurance Claims – Submit disability, cancer, accident, critical illness, hospital indemnity, and life insurance claims online
  • Track Claim Status – Monitor your submitted claims from filing through payment
  • View Policy Details – Access your insurance coverage details, benefit amounts, and policy documents
  • Enroll in Benefits – Complete open enrollment or make qualifying life event changes to your coverage
  • Access Documents & Forms – Download claim forms, policy certificates, benefits guides, and tax documents
  • Update Your Information – Manage beneficiaries, contact details, and payment preferences
  • Contact Support – Reach customer service directly through the portal

How to Sign In to Your American Fidelity Account

  1. Visit secured-americanfidelity.com in your browser
  2. Click "Sign In" in the top right corner
  3. Enter your username and password provided during registration
  4. Complete identity verification if prompted
  5. Access your dashboard with coverage summary, recent claims, and quick links

First-time user? Register here. You'll need your employee ID, date of birth, and email on file with your employer.

How to File a Claim

  1. Sign in at secured-americanfidelity.com
  2. Go to Claims from your dashboard
  3. Select your claim type (disability, cancer, accident, critical illness, hospital, or life)
  4. Complete the form with dates, diagnosis, treatment, and provider details
  5. Upload documentation (medical records, doctor statements, receipts)
  6. Submit and track — claims are typically processed within 5-10 business days

Need help? Visit our Claims Help page or call 800-654-8489.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to my American Fidelity account?

Visit secured-americanfidelity.com and click Sign In. Enter your username and password. First-time users should register here.

What is secured-americanfidelity.com?

secured-americanfidelity.com is the official online portal for American Fidelity employee benefits, providing secure access to insurance policies, claims filing, enrollment, documents, and account management.

What types of insurance does American Fidelity offer?

Supplemental insurance including Short-Term Disability (STD), Long-Term Disability (LTD), Cancer, Accident, Critical Illness, Hospital Indemnity, Life, and AD&D — all through employer-sponsored programs.

How do I file a disability claim?

Sign in, go to Claims, select Disability, complete the form with diagnosis and physician details, upload documentation, and submit. Track your claim online or call 800-654-8489.

How do I check my claim status?

Sign in to secured-americanfidelity.com and go to the Claims section to see status updates for all submitted claims including pending, in review, approved, and paid.

When is open enrollment?

Open enrollment varies by employer, typically in the fall. During this period you can add, change, or drop coverage. Contact your HR department for specific dates.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Click "Forgot Password" on the Sign In page and enter your email. Contact 800-654-8489 if you need further help.

What is the difference between STD and LTD?

STD covers temporary disabilities (weeks to months, shorter elimination period). LTD covers extended disabilities (months to years, starts after STD ends, longer elimination period).

Can I change benefits outside of open enrollment?

Only with a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, loss of other coverage, job change) within 30 days of the event.

How do I contact customer support?

Call 800-654-8489 (Mon–Fri, 7 AM – 6 PM CT), email support@secured-americanfidelity.com, or visit our Contact Us page.
