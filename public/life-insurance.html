American Fidelity Life Insurance & AD&D Coverage

American Fidelity life insurance provides a death benefit to your named beneficiaries if you pass away while covered. Options include basic employer-paid life, voluntary supplemental life, dependent life for your spouse and children, and accidental death & dismemberment (AD&D).

Types of Life Insurance Available

Basic Life Insurance

Many employers provide a basic life insurance benefit at no cost to employees, typically equal to one or two times your annual salary, up to a specified maximum. This is often automatic — no enrollment required.

Supplemental (Voluntary) Life Insurance

You can purchase additional life insurance beyond the basic employer benefit. Supplemental life lets you choose a coverage amount — usually in increments of $10,000 up to a plan maximum — with premiums based on your age and coverage amount.

Dependent Life Insurance

Coverage for your spouse and eligible dependent children. Spouse coverage is typically available in increments up to a maximum, while child coverage is a flat amount per child.

Accidental Death & Dismemberment (AD&D)

AD&D insurance pays an additional benefit if death or a covered loss (such as loss of a limb, sight, hearing, or speech) results from a covered accident. It is separate from basic life insurance and often available at low cost.

Term Life vs. Whole Life Insurance

Term life insurance covers you for a set period and has no cash value. Whole life insurance covers you permanently and builds cash value over time. Employer-sponsored group life through American Fidelity is typically term coverage that ends when you leave your employer. See the full comparison →

Naming Beneficiaries

Your beneficiary is the person or entity who receives the death benefit. You can name primary and contingent (backup) beneficiaries and update them anytime through the benefits portal. Keep beneficiaries current after major life events — marriage, divorce, birth of a child, or death of a named beneficiary.

Evidence of Insurability (EOI)

If you elect coverage above the guaranteed issue amount (the amount you can get without medical questions), you may need to provide Evidence of Insurability — a health questionnaire or medical exam. EOI is also required if you enroll in supplemental life after your initial eligibility period.

Enrollment

Enroll during open enrollment or within 30 days of your hire date. Basic life is typically automatic. Supplemental life, dependent life, and AD&D require active election.
