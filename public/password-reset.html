Reset Your American Fidelity Password

If you forgot your American Fidelity password, follow the steps below to create a new one and regain access to your benefits account.

How to Reset Your Password

  1. Go to secured-americanfidelity.com
  2. Click Forgot Password below the sign-in form
  3. Enter your username or the email address associated with your account
  4. Check your email for a password reset link — it expires after 24 hours
  5. Click the link and create a new password
  6. Sign in with your new password

Password Requirements

  • At least 8 characters
  • At least one uppercase letter and one lowercase letter
  • At least one number
  • No spaces
  • Cannot be the same as your previous three passwords

Did Not Receive the Email?

  • Check your spam or junk folder
  • Make sure the email address on your account is current
  • Allow up to 10 minutes for delivery
  • If you still do not receive it, call 800-654-8489

Also Forgot Your Username?

If you do not remember your username, visit the username recovery page first, then come back here to reset your password.

Need immediate help? Call 800-654-8489 (Mon–Fri, 7 AM – 6 PM CT).
