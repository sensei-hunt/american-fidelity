Set Up Direct Deposit for Claim Payments

American Fidelity can send your claim payments directly to your bank account. Direct deposit is faster than waiting for a check and eliminates the risk of a lost or delayed mailing.

How to Set Up Direct Deposit

  1. Sign in to your American Fidelity benefits account
  2. Go to Account Settings or Payment Preferences
  3. Select Direct Deposit
  4. Enter your bank's routing number and your account number
  5. Choose checking or savings
  6. Confirm and save

How Long Until Payments Arrive?

  • Direct deposit: typically 2–3 business days after claim approval
  • Paper check: typically 7–10 business days after claim approval

Things to Know

  • Direct deposit setup applies to all future claim payments on your account
  • You can change your bank information at any time through your account settings
  • If your bank rejects the deposit (wrong account number, closed account), American Fidelity will mail a check instead
  • Initial setup may require one payment cycle to take effect
