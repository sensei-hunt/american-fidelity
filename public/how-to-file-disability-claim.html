How to File a Disability Claim with American Fidelity

To file a disability claim, notify your employer, gather your medical documentation and attending physician statement, then sign in to the portal to complete and submit the claim form. Claims are typically processed within 5–10 business days after all documents are received.

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Notify Your Employer

Contact your HR department or benefits coordinator as soon as you know you'll need to file a disability claim. They will confirm your coverage status, provide employer-section paperwork, and may need to verify your last day worked and salary information.

Step 2: Gather Required Documents

You will need:

  • Attending Physician Statement (APS) — your doctor completes this form documenting your diagnosis, treatment plan, and expected disability duration
  • Medical records — clinical notes, test results, imaging reports supporting your condition
  • Employer verification — last day worked, salary, benefits elections
  • Other insurance information — workers' compensation, Social Security disability, or other disability benefits you may be receiving

Step 3: Sign In to the Portal

Sign in at secured-americanfidelity.com and navigate to the Claims section from your dashboard or the main menu.

Step 4: Select Your Claim Type

Choose Short-Term Disability (STD) if your disability is expected to last weeks to months, or Long-Term Disability (LTD) if your condition will extend beyond your STD benefit period. Not sure which? See STD vs. LTD.

Step 5: Complete the Claim Form

Fill out all required fields accurately, including:

  • Date your disability began
  • Last date you worked
  • Diagnosis and symptoms
  • Treating physician name, address, and phone number
  • Whether you are receiving any other disability benefits
  • Expected return-to-work date (if known)

Step 6: Upload Documentation

Attach your Attending Physician Statement, medical records, and any supporting documentation. Upload clear, legible documents in PDF or image format. Incomplete documentation is the most common reason for claims delays.

Step 7: Submit and Track

Review everything for accuracy, then submit. Save your confirmation number. Check claim status anytime by signing in and going to the Claims section. You'll see status updates: Received → Under Review → Approved/Denied → Payment Issued.

Important Tips

  • File promptly — most plans require claims within 30–90 days of disability onset
  • Be thorough — incomplete forms are the #1 cause of delays
  • Keep copies — save everything you submit for your records
  • Follow up — if you haven't heard back within 10 business days, call 800-654-8489
  • Continuing claims — for LTD, you may need to submit periodic updates on your condition

Claim Denied?

If your claim is denied, the denial letter will explain the reason and your appeal rights. Common reasons include insufficient medical documentation, pre-existing condition exclusions, or filing outside the deadline. You have the right to appeal and can submit additional documentation to support your case.

Need help with your claim? Call 800-654-8489 (Mon–Fri, 7 AM – 6 PM CT) or visit Contact Us.
