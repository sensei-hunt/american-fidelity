American Fidelity vs. Colonial Life: Supplemental Insurance Compared

American Fidelity and Colonial Life (a Unum Group company) both provide employer-sponsored supplemental insurance. American Fidelity focuses on education and public-sector employers with deep Section 125 plan expertise, while Colonial Life serves a broad range of private and public employers through its face-to-face enrollment model.

Key Differences

Enrollment Experience

Colonial Life is known for one-on-one enrollment meetings with benefits counselors who walk employees through their options in person. American Fidelity also uses benefits counselors but places stronger emphasis on online enrollment and benefits education through employer partnerships.

Employer Focus

American Fidelity has built deep relationships in education (K-12 and higher education) and public-sector employers. Colonial Life serves a wider range of industries including private-sector companies of all sizes.

Parent Company Resources

Colonial Life's parent Unum Group is one of the largest disability insurance providers in the world, giving Colonial Life access to broader underwriting resources and long-term disability products through Unum's portfolio.

Bottom Line

Both offer strong supplemental insurance products. Your employer chooses which carrier to partner with. If your employer offers American Fidelity, manage your benefits at secured-americanfidelity.com.
