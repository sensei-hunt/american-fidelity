Common Reasons to Contact Us

  • Account access — locked accounts, password resets, registration issues
  • Claims — filing a new claim, checking claim status, appealing a denial
  • Enrollment — open enrollment questions, qualifying life events, coverage changes
  • Policy information — coverage details, beneficiary updates, premium questions
  • Billing — payroll deduction questions, premium payments, payment history
  • Documents — requesting policy copies, tax forms, or Explanation of Benefits

Before You Call

To help us assist you quickly, have the following ready:

  • Your full name as it appears on your policy
  • Your policy number or employer name
  • The email address associated with your account
  • A brief description of your question or issue
