Recover Your American Fidelity Username

If you forgot the username you use to sign in to your American Fidelity benefits account, follow these steps to recover it.

How to Recover Your Username

  1. Go to secured-americanfidelity.com
  2. Click Forgot Username below the sign-in form
  3. Enter your email address or policy number
  4. Check your email — your username will be sent to the address on file
  5. Return to the sign-in page and log in

Where to Find Your Policy Number

Your American Fidelity policy number appears on:

  • Your benefits enrollment confirmation
  • Your insurance certificate or policy document
  • Payroll stubs (if premiums are deducted)
  • Correspondence from American Fidelity

Never Registered?

If you have not created an online account yet, you will need to register first. Your username is created during registration.

Still cannot find your username? Call 800-654-8489 (Mon–Fri, 7 AM – 6 PM CT).
