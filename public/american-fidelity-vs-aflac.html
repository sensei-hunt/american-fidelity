American Fidelity vs. Aflac: Supplemental Insurance Compared

Both American Fidelity and Aflac offer supplemental insurance through employer benefit programs, but they differ in focus areas, distribution models, and product design. American Fidelity specializes in education and public-sector employers with strong Section 125 plan administration, while Aflac is a larger, publicly traded company known for individual and worksite marketing.

Key Differences

Section 125 Expertise

American Fidelity is one of the leading administrators of Section 125 cafeteria plans, helping employers set up pre-tax payroll deductions for supplemental benefits. This can reduce both employer and employee tax liability. Aflac relies more on post-tax individual policies.

Claims Process

Both companies offer online claims filing. American Fidelity provides claims support through its online portal and phone line. Aflac offers a mobile app (SmartClaim) for certain claim types with one-day processing for some claims.

Employer Focus

American Fidelity has deep expertise in education, public sector, and healthcare employers. Aflac serves a broader range of employer sizes and industries and also sells individual policies outside of the employer channel.

Which Is Right for You?

Your employer chooses which supplemental insurance carrier to offer. If your employer offers American Fidelity, you access your benefits through the secured-americanfidelity.com portal. You typically cannot switch carriers on your own — this is an employer-level decision.
