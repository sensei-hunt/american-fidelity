American Fidelity Disability Insurance: STD & LTD Coverage

American Fidelity disability insurance replaces a portion of your income — typically 60–70% of your base salary — when a covered illness or injury prevents you from working. Coverage is available as short-term disability (STD) for temporary conditions and long-term disability (LTD) for extended disabilities.

What Is Disability Insurance?

Disability insurance is a supplemental benefit that provides regular cash payments when you cannot perform your job duties due to a medical condition. Unlike workers' compensation, disability insurance covers both work-related and non-work-related illnesses and injuries. American Fidelity offers disability coverage through employer-sponsored benefit programs, often with pre-tax payroll deductions under a Section 125 cafeteria plan.

Short-Term Disability (STD) vs. Long-Term Disability (LTD)

STD covers temporary disabilities lasting a few weeks to several months with a short waiting period (0–14 days). LTD covers extended disabilities that last months or years, beginning after STD benefits expire, with a longer waiting period (90–180 days). See the full STD vs. LTD comparison →

What Does Disability Insurance Cover?

American Fidelity disability insurance covers conditions that prevent you from performing the material duties of your occupation, including:

  • Illnesses — cancer, heart disease, stroke, mental health conditions, chronic conditions
  • Injuries — back injuries, fractures, joint replacements, traumatic injuries
  • Surgery recovery — planned surgeries and post-operative recovery periods
  • Pregnancy and childbirth — maternity leave coverage (STD)
  • Musculoskeletal disorders — herniated discs, carpal tunnel, fibromyalgia

How Much Does Disability Insurance Pay?

Benefit amounts depend on your plan selection and salary. Most American Fidelity disability plans pay between 60% and 70% of your pre-disability earnings, up to a monthly maximum defined in your policy. Benefits are typically paid directly to you, not your healthcare provider.

Example Benefit Calculation

If your annual salary is $50,000 and your STD plan pays 66.67%:

  • Monthly pre-disability income: $4,167
  • Monthly STD benefit: ~$2,778
  • Paid for the duration specified in your plan (typically 13–26 weeks)

The Elimination Period

The elimination period is the waiting time between when your disability begins and when benefits start. Think of it like a deductible, but measured in days instead of dollars. You must be continuously disabled throughout the elimination period before benefits begin.

  • STD elimination period: Usually 0–14 days for illness; 0 days for accidents
  • LTD elimination period: Usually 90 or 180 days, often coordinated to start when STD benefits end

How to Enroll in Disability Insurance

  1. Sign in to your American Fidelity benefits portal

  3. Select your STD and/or LTD coverage level
