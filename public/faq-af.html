Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about your American Fidelity benefits, account access, and claims. Can't find what you need? Contact our support team.

Account Access

How do I access my American Fidelity account online?

Visit secured-americanfidelity.com and click "Sign In." Enter your username (email address) and password. If you haven't registered yet, click "Register" to create your account using your policy number and personal information.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the sign-in page, click "Forgot Password" and enter the email address associated with your account. You'll receive a password reset link within a few minutes. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder or call 800-654-8489.

Can I access my account on my phone?

Yes. The American Fidelity benefits portal is fully responsive and works on all modern smartphones and tablets. Simply visit secured-americanfidelity.com from your mobile browser and sign in as usual.

Benefits & Enrollment

What types of insurance does American Fidelity offer?

American Fidelity offers supplemental insurance products including disability insurance, cancer insurance, accident insurance, hospital indemnity insurance, and life insurance. Visit our Benefits Guide for detailed information about each coverage type.

When is open enrollment?

Open enrollment dates vary by employer. Contact your HR department or benefits coordinator for your specific enrollment window. You can also enroll or make changes within 31 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage, birth of a child, or loss of other coverage.

Can I change my coverage after enrollment?

Coverage changes are generally limited to open enrollment periods or qualifying life events. Some plans may allow you to increase coverage at annual re-enrollment. Contact your benefits coordinator or American Fidelity for details about your specific plan.

Are my premiums deducted pre-tax?

In most cases, yes. When offered through your employer's Section 125 cafeteria plan, your premiums are deducted from your paycheck before taxes are calculated, reducing your overall tax burden. Check with your employer to confirm your plan's tax treatment.

Claims

How do I file a claim?

Sign in to your account and navigate to the Claims section. Select your claim type, complete the form, upload supporting documents, and submit. For detailed instructions, visit our Claims Help page. You can also file by calling 800-654-8489.

How long does it take to process a claim?

Processing times vary by claim type. Accident claims are typically processed within 5-10 business days. Disability and cancer claims usually take 10-15 business days. Timelines depend on the completeness of your documentation.

How will I receive my claim payment?

You can choose direct deposit (fastest, 2-3 business days after approval) or a paper check mailed to your address on file (7-10 business days). Set up your payment preference in your account settings.

What if my claim is denied?

You'll receive a written explanation of the denial reason. You have the right to appeal within 60 days by submitting additional documentation. See our Claims Help page for details on the appeals process.
