Understanding Your American Fidelity Employee Benefits

American Fidelity has been helping employees protect what matters most for over 60 years. As an employee enrolled in American Fidelity benefits, you have access to a range of supplemental insurance products designed to provide financial protection when you need it most — covering gaps that your primary health insurance may not.

What Is Supplemental Insurance?

Supplemental insurance provides additional financial protection beyond your employer's primary health plan. While your major medical insurance covers doctor visits and hospital stays, supplemental insurance pays cash benefits directly to you when a covered event occurs — such as an accident, a serious illness diagnosis, or a disability that prevents you from working.

Types of Coverage Available

Disability Insurance: Replaces a portion of your income if you become unable to work due to illness or injury. Short-term disability typically covers 60-70% of your salary for a limited period, giving you financial stability while you recover.

Cancer Insurance: Provides a lump-sum benefit or scheduled payments upon diagnosis of cancer. Benefits can help cover treatment costs, travel to specialists, and everyday living expenses during recovery.

Accident Insurance: Pays cash benefits for injuries resulting from covered accidents, including fractures, dislocations, emergency room visits, and hospital stays.

Life Insurance: Provides financial protection for your loved ones. American Fidelity offers both term and whole life options with flexible coverage amounts.

Hospital Indemnity Insurance: Pays a fixed daily benefit for each day you're hospitalized, helping offset high deductibles and out-of-pocket costs.

How to Enroll

Enrollment is available during your employer's annual open enrollment period or within 31 days of a qualifying life event. Your HR department can provide specific enrollment dates.

  • Review available plan options and coverage levels
  • Consider your family's health history and financial situation
  • Choose coverage levels that fit your budget — premiums are deducted pre-tax
  • Complete enrollment through your benefits portal or during a scheduled meeting

Making the Most of Your Benefits

  • File claims promptly — don't let eligible benefits go uncollected
  • Keep copies of all medical records and bills related to covered events
  • Review your coverage annually during open enrollment
  • Update your beneficiaries after any major life change
  • Contact American Fidelity if you're unsure whether an event is covered
